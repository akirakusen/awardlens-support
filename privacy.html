AwardLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2026

AwardLens uses your information to provide account access, mileage-estimate search, route insights, feedback handling, security, and abuse prevention.

Information We Collect

  • Account information from Apple or Google sign-in, such as email address when provided, provider user identifier, internal user identifier, account status, access level, and optional display name.
  • Search and usage activity, such as route, cabin, endpoint, status code, app version, client installation identifier hash, IP hash, and user-agent hash.
  • Feedback you choose to send, including feedback text and related route, program, or result context.
  • On-device app data, such as session tokens stored in Keychain and recent searches or cache data stored locally on your device.

How We Use Information

  • To authenticate your account and keep access tied to Apple or Google sign-in.
  • To provide mileage-estimate search, route insights, and program notes.
  • To improve data quality, troubleshoot issues, monitor service health, and prevent abuse.
  • To respond to support and feedback requests.

Sharing

AwardLens does not sell personal information and does not use advertising tracking. The app contacts Apple or Google for sign-in when you choose those providers. Service infrastructure may process operational data needed to run the app.

Retention and Deletion

Account deletion disables login access and removes personal profile details. Feedback and operational usage logs may be retained for data quality, security, and abuse-prevention purposes.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email juicernchu@outlook.com.
