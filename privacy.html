AwardLens Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026. This policy describes the free, native AwardLens app.

AwardLens compares award-redemption references and provides mileage-program transfer information. It does not require an AwardLens account or a purchase, sell tickets, transfer points, or check live award availability.

On Your Device

Route and mileage-budget searches, recent searches, personal point acquisition costs, preferences, and downloaded catalogs are processed on your device. Searches and saved point costs are not sent to an AwardLens backend. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, collect advertising identifiers, or request your device location.

The catalog contains travel references, not user accounts. Local preferences and file metadata are used to save app settings and safely load catalog files, not to identify or track people.

Network Services

The app checks public HTTPS files hosted by GitHub and its delivery providers for catalog updates. Those services receive ordinary network information, such as your IP address and request metadata. Requests do not include your route search, an AwardLens account, a private GitHub token or an Apple purchase identity.

Apple MapKit requests map content from Apple using the map area being displayed. Opening a source link takes you to an external website. These providers handle requests under their own policies: GitHub Privacy Statement and Apple Privacy Policy.

Optional Support Email

If you choose to email support, your email address and the information you include are shared with the support recipient and email providers. We use the correspondence to respond to your request. Do not include passwords, payment information or booking documents. You can contact support to request deletion of that correspondence.

Deletion and Historical Records

Deleting the app removes its on-device catalog and preferences. Offloading the app keeps its data. Backups and restores follow your Apple device and backup settings.

The current app has no account to delete. Earlier account-based versions and their backend were retired. Updating or deleting the app does not erase historical account records, private recovery backups or support email. Contact support with questions or a deletion request concerning those records.

Contact and Changes

For privacy or support questions, email awardlens.contact@gmail.com. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
