AwardLens Support

AwardLens is a planning and research app for comparing premium-cabin mileage redemption cost estimates from Singapore.

Contact

For support, account help, or App Store review questions, email juicernchu@outlook.com.

Account Deletion

You can request account deletion in the app from Account > Account controls. You can also email support from the address associated with your Apple or Google sign-in.

Deleting your account disables login access and removes personal profile details. Feedback and operational usage logs may be retained for data quality, security, and abuse-prevention review.

Important Notes

AwardLens provides mileage redemption estimates for research. It does not sell tickets, transfer points, or guarantee award availability. Always confirm taxes, fees, availability, and booking rules with the relevant airline or loyalty program.

AwardLens is independent and is not affiliated with airlines, alliances, or loyalty programs.
